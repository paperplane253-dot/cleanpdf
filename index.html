About Cleanpdf

A free, privacy-first tool for removing hidden data from PDF files — built for journalists, lawyers, researchers, and anyone who cares about what they share.

What is PDF metadata?

Every PDF you create carries invisible data baked in by the software that made it. This metadata can reveal who wrote the document, when it was created, what software and operating system was used, and sometimes even the GPS coordinates of where it was created.

This information is invisible when you read or print the file — but it's easy for anyone to extract. Sending a PDF without stripping its metadata can inadvertently expose personal or organisational information you never intended to share.

How Cleanpdf works

Unlike most tools that upload your file to a server, Cleanpdf processes everything directly in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Your PDF is read into memory, parsed, stripped, and returned to you — without ever leaving your device.

🔒
Fully private
No uploads, no servers, no logs. Your file exists only in your browser tab.
⚡
Instant results
Processing happens in milliseconds, even for large files, with no waiting for a server response.
🔍
Full transparency
Every metadata field is shown before removal so you know exactly what was in your file.
🧬
Alteration detection
SHA-256 fingerprinting lets you verify whether a PDF was modified after you cleaned it.

Who should use this?

Anyone sending PDFs to people they don't fully trust. Common use cases include journalists protecting sources, lawyers sanitising documents before disclosure, HR teams removing internal system data from job offers, researchers sharing drafts without revealing authorship, and individuals who simply value their privacy.
